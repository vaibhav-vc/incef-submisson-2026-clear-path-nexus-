# Hazard log (draft for Indian Railways review)

Scope: Clear Path Nexus / RailGuard as an **advisory** decision-support tool for section controllers and an
advisory cab display. It has no interface to signalling, interlocking, points, Kavach/ATP or brakes. Hazards are
therefore about *wrong or misleading advice* and *loss of the advice*, never about direct control.

This is a draft prepared by the developer in the EN 50126 style, for IR's safety organisation to review, re-score
and own. Severity and likelihood are the developer's initial estimates. **Residual risk acceptance is IR's
decision.**

Severity: Catastrophic / Critical / Marginal / Negligible. Likelihood (with mitigations): Frequent / Probable /
Occasional / Remote / Improbable.

| ID | Hazard | Causes | Possible consequence if acted on without other barriers | Mitigations in the design | Verification evidence | Residual (dev. estimate) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H01 | A ranked plan contains a conflict (two trains planned too close) | Planner defect; stale twin state; horizon limits | Controller sets a conflicting path; protected by signalling/interlocking, but delay or unsafe pressure on staff | Conflict search against every occupation incl. the occupied section; pair-symmetric 240-min horizon; joint re-check of all re-pathed trains; later conflicts raised as WARNING threats | Simulation invariants SAFE_SEPARATION and RANKING: 0 violations in the final runs on real data (3.19M operations; 9.87M across all runs); `tests/test_national.py` | Marginal / Improbable | Open for IR review |
| H02 | Advice based on stale or wrong train positions | Feed outage; delayed data; GNSS error; spoofing | Plan assumes trains are elsewhere | EvidenceGate: stale → HOLD, missing → UNAVAILABLE; projections are PLANNING_ONLY and cannot be approved as live; signed feed with replay protection; map-matching rejects off-route fixes; implausible jumps rejected | EVIDENCE_GATE / INPUT_REJECTION invariants; `tests/test_livefeed.py` | Marginal / Remote | Open |
| H03 | Approval of an outdated recommendation | State changes between ranking and approval | Plan no longer fits the situation | Version check: any change (clock, position, section, hold, threat) supersedes the ranking | APPROVAL_GATE invariant; `test_superseded_*` tests | Marginal / Improbable | Open |
| H04 | Plan routes a train over a closed or obstructed section | Late infrastructure input; planner defect | Train sent towards a blocked line (stopped by signals) | Hard feasibility filter for every option; SECTION_CLOSED / OBSTACLE critical threats block approval until acknowledged; cab shows HOLD-FOR-CONTROLLER | RANKING / CAB_ADVISORY invariants | Marginal / Improbable | Open |
| H05 | Driver treats the cab advisory as movement authority | Human factors; display design | Driver proceeds against signals | Permanent footer "authorised signals, rules and ATP remain controlling"; no speed band without fresh evidence and an approved plan; status degrades to DATA UNAVAILABLE | CAB_ADVISORY invariant; UI review | Critical / Remote (human factors: needs IR trial) | Open: needs human-factors assessment |
| H06 | Loss of the advisory service during operation | Server failure; network loss; overload | Controllers lose decision support | Advisory only: normal working continues without it; cab shows DATA UNAVAILABLE on link loss; rate limits; bounded memory; 60 s liveness watchdog in simulation | LIVENESS invariant; `test_rate_limit` | Negligible / Occasional | Open |
| H07 | Unauthorised person issues approvals or injects data | Credential theft; network attack | False plans or false positions | Role tokens (viewer / controller / feed), fail-closed production mode, host allow-list, strict schemas, signed feed, audit chain with HMAC | `tests/test_security.py`, `tests/test_attacks.py`, `tests/test_attacks_live.py` (41 cases), Bandit 0, pip-audit 0 | Marginal / Remote | Open: needs external audit |
| H08 | Decision record altered after the fact | Insider tampering | Investigation misled | Hash-chained, HMAC-signed, persisted audit; CLI verification; snapshots replay to the same ranking | `test_audit_chain_detects_tampering_and_survives_restart`; REPLAY invariant | Negligible / Remote | Open |
| H09 | Forecast of a late train is wrong | Model error; unusual days (fog, blocks) outside the training month | Conflicts looked for in the wrong place; optimistic or pessimistic advice | Forecast learned from 1.26M *actual* arrivals and scored forward in time on unseen days (13.9 min average error vs 17.4 for the previous rule; P10-P90 band covered 82%); it moves only the projection of feed-reported trains; it can never run a train faster than 85% of timetabled time or leave a stop before arriving; it never replaces a controller's decision; stale feed still fails closed to HOLD | `evidence/real_data/real_validation.json`; `test_forecast_plans_never_overlap_*`, `test_a_forecast_never_replaces_*`; RANKING invariant | Marginal / Occasional | Open: retrain on IR's own feed across seasons |
| H10 | Infrastructure attributes are wrong | Volunteer-mapped OSM; no block-section, loop or signal data in public sources | Single line read as double (missed opposing conflicts) or double as single (false warnings) | Line count from OpenStreetMap on 90% of sections, counted conservatively (one single-line stretch over 2 km makes the section single; only accepted paths that agree with the timetable's rail distance are used); unknown → single line; station positions from the map by IR code; every attribute labelled with its evidence; checked against known single and double routes | `tests/test_osm_infra.py`; `evidence/real_data/real_validation.json` (track_data) | Marginal / Occasional | Open: needs IR engineering register (block sections, loops) |
| H11 | Train shown as running on a day it does not run | Running days missing or changed | Phantom conflicts / missed conflicts | Running days of every 2024 train taken from the days it was actually observed running (daily if seen on 26+ of 30 days; a train never observed is treated as daily, the safe side); official days override when ingested; live feed overrides projections | `test_twin_runs_trains_only_on_their_days`, `test_build_derives_running_days_*` | Marginal / Remote | Open: confirm with the current official timetable |
| H12 | Real feed reports rejected or misapplied | Late train projected ahead of its real position; reports out of order or for unknown runs | Real positions ignored (twin keeps a wrong projection) or a train moved backwards | Reports matched from the last accepted observation, never from the projection; reports behind it refused as out of order; replay of a real morning: 96% accepted, the rest out-of-order or unknown runs, refused on purpose | `test_reports_are_matched_from_the_last_observation_*`; `evidence/real_data/real_validation.json` (live_feed_replay) | Marginal / Remote | Open: confirm with the CRIS feed in its test environment |
| H13 | Source timetable contains placeholder times | Every stop listed at the same minute (343 trains in the 2024 file) | Phantom trains occupying sections at one instant; meaningless delays | Detected and excluded from the twin and from all scoring; counted in the build report | `test_build_derives_running_days_and_actual_times_and_drops_placeholder_timetables` | Negligible / Remote | Closed for this source; re-check every new timetable |
| H14 | Conflict warnings do not match reality | Section data between stopping stations cannot see block sections, loops or signals | Controllers distrust or over-trust warnings | Warnings shown as advice with their basis; measured on real days (lift over comparable trains reported, not hidden); the shadow trial compares them with controllers' decisions | `evidence/real_data/real_validation.json` (conflicts) | Marginal / Occasional | Open: IR block-section and loop data; shadow trial |
| H15 | A GNSS position is spoofed, reflected (multipath) or reported for the wrong train | RF spoofing/jamming; cuttings and urban canyons; cab unit mis-configured with another train number | Twin places the train where it is not; conflicts missed or invented | Receiver quality gates; map-matching to the mapped track of the planned route (yards 300 m, mapped line max(50 m, 3x accuracy)); implausible backward/fast moves refused and raised as GNSS_IMPLAUSIBLE; far-off fixes raise ROUTE_DEVIATION at once, near ones on the second in a row; every refusal leaves the old evidence to age to HOLD | GNSS_GATE invariant; `tests/test_gps.py`; real-network check: 99.66% genuine accepted, 100% jumps/teleports refused, 0.02% false alarms (`evidence/gnss/`) | Marginal / Remote | Open: field trial with RTIS / cab units |
| H16 | Mains power fails during operation | Grid failure at the control site | Decisions half-written; state lost | UPS monitored through NUT; on battery: warning to all consoles, checkpoint every 10 s; low battery: approvals paused, readiness fails so users move to the standby; signed checkpoints restored on the standby | `tests/test_ops.py`; container restart test | Negligible / Occasional | Open: IR site UPS and standby site |
| H17 | Stale or forged state restored after a restart | Old checkpoint; storage tampering | Twin starts from a wrong picture | Restore only if HMAC valid, same timetable data and younger than 30 min; restored evidence keeps its timestamps and ages to STALE; restore recorded in the audit chain | `test_a_tampered_stale_or_foreign_checkpoint_is_not_restored` | Negligible / Remote | Open |
| H18 | A cab advisory reaches the wrong cab | Token copied or unit moved to another loco | Driver sees another train's advisory | Run-scoped tokens: one run, 1-24 h, issued per unit and audited; the cab screen holds only that capability (issued as a link whose fragment never reaches a server) and receives pushes; a link silent for 25 s shows DATA UNAVAILABLE with no speed band; the advisory carries its train number and footer; revocation by key rotation | `tests/test_live.py`, `tests/test_attacks_live.py`; Chromium: console-issued link, push to the cab, forged link refused, silent link blanked after 24.9 s | Marginal / Remote | Open: issue procedure with IR |
| H19 | Decision taken under a shared or stolen identity | Shared controller token; password guessing | No accountability; unauthorised decision | Named accounts with lockout and hashed sessions; accounts-only decisions (`RAILGUARD_REQUIRE_ACCOUNTS=1`); audit records the person | `tests/test_accounts.py`, `tests/test_attacks_live.py` | Marginal / Remote | Open: external audit; IR identity (SSO) integration |
| H20 | A freight path or a delay-advisor finding is read as an instruction | Human factors | Wrong path set or wrong works priority | Both are labelled advisory; freight paths pass an independent conflict check before they are shown (500 if not); advisor findings state "where to look first", the lever, the minutes at stake and their persistence, never a command | `tests/test_freight.py`, `tests/test_advisor.py` | Negligible / Occasional | Open: operator training |
