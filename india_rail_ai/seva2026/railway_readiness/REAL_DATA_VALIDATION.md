# Verification on real-life data

Generated by `python -m india_rail real validate` from `seva2026/evidence/real_data/real_validation.json`.
Every number below is computed from what actually happened, not from data this project generated.

## Data
* **Actual running:** 56,395 train runs, 1,262,418 actual arrival times, 3,549 trains, 2024-09-01 to 2024-09-30. Source: Chowdhury, Koley, Chakraborty, Ghosh - IIT Kharagpur (IEEE T-ITS 2026, arXiv:2510.01262). No licence stated; published for research with a citation request. Not redistributed.
* **Timetable:** September 2024, 3,549 trains, 75,343 timed sections; 343 trains with placeholder times excluded.
* **Running days:** observed for all 3,549 trains (1,160 daily).
* **Track data (OpenStreetMap, ODbL):** 8,224 stations located by their IR code; 8,188 of 9,065 sections mapped: 3,862 single line, 4,325 double or more; 7,830 at least 90% electrified.

## 1. Is the data credible?
Mail/Express trains at destination within 15 minutes: **81.97%** observed in September 2024; the Ministry of Railways published **77.12%** for 2024-25 (financial year), which includes the winter fog months. A credible sample of real running.

## 2. How old was the open timetable?
Of 862 trains with the same number, origin and destination in 2016 and 2024, 64.2% changed journey time by more than 15 minutes; 6,091 sections of 2024 are not in the 2016 data. The twin now runs on the 2024 timetable.

The deployed model (`models/eta_model.joblib`) is the one scored here; it is rebuilt locally by this command and, like the data it learned from, never distributed.

## 4. Do conflict warnings come true?
At six times of day on four unseen days the national twin was given every train's real delay and flagged conflicts in the next two hours. A correct warning predicts that the train which must give way loses time on that section; it is compared with unflagged trains in the same state (late or not).

| Twin | Warnings | Give-way train lost >=5 min | Comparable trains | Lift |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Previous projection, no track data | 14,044 | 27.33% | 24.74% | 1.1x |
| Previous projection + real track data | 7,811 | 27.49% | 25.01% | 1.1x |
| Learned projection + real track data | 8,164 | 28.67% | 24.63% | 1.16x |

Warnings are informative but far from certain: sections between stopping stations cannot show block sections, intermediate loops or signals. Indian Railways' engineering registers and COA data are the next real gain (hazard H14).

## 6. Real time lost per section
Single line: 23.81% of traversals lost 5+ minutes; double line or more: 24.79%. Busy double-line trunk routes lose time as often as single lines.

## Limits
One month (September, no fog season). Only arrivals are observed; departures in the file are arrival plus scheduled halt. Early running is recorded as zero. Freight and suburban trains are not included. OpenStreetMap is volunteer-mapped. A shadow trial on live data with controllers is still needed.
